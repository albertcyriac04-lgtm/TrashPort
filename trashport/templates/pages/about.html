About TrashPort

Transforming Urban Waste Management
Through Smart Logistics

TrashPort is an end-to-end, on-demand smart waste management and recycling platform designed to modernize urban sanitation — bridging citizens, collectors, and municipal authorities through real-time intelligence.

Our Mission

We aim to build a cleaner, more sustainable urban ecosystem by making waste collection transparent, efficient, and rewarding. By incentivizing citizens to recycle and empowering collectors with fair, automated earnings, we are revolutionizing how cities handle their daily waste footprint — eliminating inefficiencies of static route scheduling through real-time demand and AI-assisted logistics.

Ecosystem Architecture

How the Ecosystem Works

Designed for seamless collaboration between citizens, collectors, and city administrators.

On-Demand & Rewarding

For Citizens

Schedule waste collection on your terms. Categorize waste, estimate weight, and earn recycling incentive points for high-value categories. Active subscribers enjoy zero-fee routine collections with our integrated Groq AI chatbot assistant.

Optimized & Fair Routing

For Collectors

Verified drivers get a live, geolocation-aware job board. Our automated dispatch engine assigns nearest jobs using proximity algorithms. On-site weight verification and automated commission calculations guarantee transparent, immediate digital payouts.

Complete Oversight

For Administrators

The Admin Control Room is the central nervous system of TrashPort. City authorities gain real-time visibility through live GPS fleet tracking, automated compliance audits, and dynamic dashboards covering revenue, recycled tonnage, and collector payouts.
